02 / About

A builder you deal with directly

I'm Stuart Freeth, a general builder based in Truro with a yard on the Roseland.

Over the last 7 years my work has focused on high end projects on the Roseland, mostly renovations and extensions, along with carpentry, concreting and groundworks.

I'm now happy to take on smaller jobs too. When you get in touch, you deal with me directly, from the first site visit to the finished job.
